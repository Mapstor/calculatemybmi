## Method changes (compute.py)
- Pounds = kg × 2.2046 (NCHS's factor; was 2.20462), percentiles from the weighted distribution over distinct values (no tie order), heights keep pregnant women as Table 8 does. With these, Table 4 in full (160 values) and the adult rows of Tables 8 and 12 and Table 10's adult BMI means and medians reproduce at 0.1 (asserted, 204 values; the fact-check reproduced all 478 means and percentiles of Tables 4, 8 and 12); the old method missed the women's 95th percentile (262.8 vs 262.7) and the men's 40–49 mean (210.2 vs 210.1).
- The tool's ±1-inch percentiles are stored at 4 decimals (they were 2, then rounded to 1 on the page: 126 of 2,673 values 0.1 lb off, nothing visible); `age_pairs_30_59` adds the 30s-vs-50s gap, so "not statistically different" covers every pair.
- The same method reruns `research/chart-men-build/bcm_stats.py`: the men's chart's middle-half ranges and medians move by 0.1–1.8 lb (the 5′10″ median 199.3 → 199.1 lb); `/blog/bmi-chart-men/` and its preferred images are regenerated (`gen_bcm.py` on its CMB-017 source, as built). No other page prints a figure that changed: the means other generators read are the same.
