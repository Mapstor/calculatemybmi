The average American man weighs 199.0 lb (90.3 kg) and the average woman 171.8 lb (77.9 kg), based on adults 20 and older measured by CDC in August 2021–August 2023. Across heights, average weight rises about 4.0 lb per inch for women and 6.2 lb for men (our calculation), but not perfectly smoothly: each height is its own sample of real women, so neighboring rows can wobble by a few pounds. Average weight vs healthy weight

At every height we can show, for both women and men, the average weight corresponds to a BMI of 25 or more. In other words, the typical American at almost any height is above the healthy-weight range. That’s why “average” and “healthy” shouldn’t be read as the same thing; for a target, our ideal weight calculator and BMI chart are the better tools.

Average weight by age

Weight peaks in middle age: men average the most at 40–49 (210.2 lb) and women at 50–59 (180.0 lb), then averages fall in older groups. These are different people at different ages in one survey, not the same people followed over time.

Average BMI, height and waist size

The average man is 68.9 inches tall (about 5′9″) with a BMI of 29.4 and a waist of 40.6 inches; the average woman is 63.5 inches (about 5′3.5″) with a BMI of 30.0 and a waist of 38.5 inches. Men’s average BMI is in CDC’s overweight range (25 to under 30); women’s sits right at the line between overweight and obesity. Half of men weigh more than 191 lb and half of women more than 162 lb: the medians are lower than the averages because the heaviest people pull averages up.

FAQ

What is the average weight for a woman?

171.8 lb (77.9 kg) for US women 20 and older, measured in 2021–2023. The median is 162 lb.

What is the average weight for a man?

199.0 lb (90.3 kg) for US men 20 and older; the median is 191 lb.

What is the average weight for a 5′4″ woman?

171.2 lb, with half of women that height between 140 and 194 lb. The healthy range at 5′4″ is 108–145 lb.

What is the average weight for a 5′9″ man?

204.0 lb, with half of men that height between 171 and 235 lb. The healthy range at 5′9″ is 125–168 lb.

What is the average waist size?

40.6 inches for men and 38.5 inches for women, measured at the top of the hip bone in CDC’s exam.

Is average weight the same as healthy weight?

No. At most heights the average corresponds to a BMI of 25 or more, which CDC classes as overweight. Use the healthy-range column or our BMI chart for that.

How we calculated this

We used CDC’s National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey for August 2021–August 2023: the demographics file and the body measures file, where trained staff weigh and measure every participant. We kept adults 20 and older, excluded pregnant women, and applied CDC’s exam sample weights so the results represent the US population. Standard errors use the survey’s strata and primary sampling units (Taylor linearization). We show a height only when at least 30 people were measured at it.

Check against CDC: our overall results reproduce CDC’s published figures in Vital and Health Statistics Series 3, No. 50 — average weight 199.0 lb for men and 171.8 lb for women, height 68.9 and 63.5 inches, BMI 29.4 and 30.0. The breakdowns by height are our own calculation from the same files.
