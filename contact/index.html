Understanding Your BMI Results

Body Mass Index can sometimes be confusing to interpret, especially when you factor in variables like age, gender, muscle mass, and athletic status. Our team can help clarify what your specific BMI number means, which weight category it places you in according to World Health Organization and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention guidelines, and what factors might affect the accuracy of this measurement for your individual situation. We can also explain the differences between our various calculators, such as the standard BMI calculator versus the Trefethen New BMI formula, which some researchers argue provides more accurate results for very tall and very short individuals.

About Our Team and Mission

BMI Calculator was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to make accurate health assessment tools freely available to everyone, regardless of their location, financial situation, or technical expertise. We believe that understanding your body mass index is an important first step in health awareness, and that this information should not be locked behind paywalls or complicated registration processes.

Our team consists of individuals passionate about health education, web development, and user experience design. We are committed to presenting health information in a way that is accessible to the general public while still maintaining scientific accuracy. We regularly review the latest research on BMI, body composition, and weight-related health outcomes to ensure that our content reflects current scientific understanding.

Every calculator on our site is developed using established medical formulas that are recognized by major health organizations including the World Health Organization (WHO), the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the National Institutes of Health (NIH). Since our launch, we have served millions of users from over 150 countries. Important Health Information Disclaimer

Please Read Before Contacting Us About Health Concerns

Our calculators and educational content are designed for general informational and educational purposes only. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic test, and it has well-documented limitations.

Our team consists of health educators and web developers, not licensed medical professionals. We cannot provide personalized medical advice, diagnose health conditions, recommend specific treatments, or interpret your results in the context of your complete health history.

If you have concerns about your weight, BMI, or overall health, please consult a qualified healthcare provider. A doctor, registered dietitian, or other licensed health professional can provide personalized guidance based on a comprehensive evaluation that includes factors our calculators cannot assess, such as your medical history, family history, blood work results, and physical examination findings.

BMI is a useful screening tool, but it should not be the sole basis for medical decisions. The BMI formula has important limitations: it does not distinguish between muscle and fat, does not account for body fat distribution, and may not be accurate for athletes, elderly individuals, or certain ethnic groups. Our standard BMI calculator uses the universally recognized formula: BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)². This is the same formula used by the World Health Organization, the CDC, and healthcare providers worldwide. We also offer a New BMI Calculator based on the Trefethen formula (BMI = 1.3 x weight (kg) / height (m)^2.5), which some researchers suggest provides more accurate results for very tall and very short individuals. You can learn more about BMI formulas in our BMI Formula Guide.

Yes! We offer a specialized Pediatric BMI Calculator designed specifically for children and teenagers between the ages of 2 and 19. Unlike adult BMI, which uses fixed category cutoffs, pediatric BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific percentiles based on CDC growth charts. This accounts for the fact that children's body composition changes as they grow. Our pediatric calculator provides BMI-for-age percentile results and explains what each percentile range means for your child's health. For more information, read our Pediatric BMI Guide.
