Contact

CalculateMyBMI.net is built and maintained by Marko Visic, MPharm, and operated by Moving Data Systems d.o.o. Email is the only support channel.

info@calculatemybmi.net

What to email about

  • Factual corrections. A number, formula, statistic, or citation on any page looks wrong — please point to the specific page and sentence.
  • Broken links or citations. An external reference no longer resolves, or its content has changed materially.
  • Technical problems. A calculator returns an unexpected result, an input does not accept a valid value, or a page will not render. Include your browser and device.
  • Accessibility issues. Something on the site is hard to use with a screen reader, keyboard, or at your zoom level.
  • Editorial questions. How a source was interpreted or why a claim is (or is not) on a page.

What we can not help with: personalised medical advice, diagnosis, or interpretation of your results in the context of your health history. See the disclaimer below.

Response time

Replies typically arrive within a few business days. Complex questions may take longer. Correction reports are prioritised.

Health information disclaimer

Everything on CalculateMyBMI.net is for general information and education. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for clinical judgement. BMI is a screening tool with well-documented limitations.

The site is written by a pharmacist (MPharm), not a physician. It cannot give personalised medical advice or interpret your numbers in the context of your health history.

If you have a concern about your weight, body composition, or health, speak to a qualified healthcare provider.

Privacy when you email

Your message and email address are used only to reply to you and, where relevant, to make the correction you reported. Nothing you send is added to a mailing list, sold, or shared with a third party.

For details on cookies, analytics, and data controller information, see the Privacy Policy.
