Key takeaways

BMI (body mass index) is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters squared, or pounds × 703 ÷ inches². For adults 20 and over, CDC uses one scale for everyone: under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is a healthy weight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or more is obesity.

  • The healthy weight depends only on height: at 5′7″ (170.2 cm) it is 118 to 159 lb (54 to 72 kg).
  • The median US adult has a BMI of 28.2, and 72.4% have a BMI of 25 or more (CDC measurements, 2021–2023).
  • Obesity has three classes: 30 to 34.9, 35 to 39.9 and 40 or more.
  • Under 20, BMI is read as a percentile for age and sex, not on this scale.
  • BMI can’t tell muscle from fat, and it can’t show where fat sits; a waist measurement can.

Medical disclaimer: This calculator gives estimates for information only. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. Ask a healthcare professional for medical advice.

How to read your result

Every number below uses the calculator’s imperial starting values: 5′7″ (170.2 cm) and 160 lb (72.6 kg). That weight sits right on the line between two categories.

BMI: 25.1, overweight
160 × 703 ÷ 67² = 25.1, rounded to one decimal; the category comes from that rounded number. At 159 lb the BMI would be 24.9, a healthy weight. The calculator shows the CDC category for adults, and below 17 the WHO thinness grades: moderate thinness from 16 to 16.9 and severe thinness under 16 (WHO, 1995).

The lightest and heaviest whole pounds at your height with a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9, and the same in whole kilograms. It depends only on height. In imperial mode the tiles also show each limit converted (53.5 and 72.1 kg).
1 lb (0.5 kg) above the healthy range
The distance from your weight to the nearest end of that range.
Compared with US adults: higher than 28%
The share of adults aged 20 and over, measured by CDC in 2021–2023, whose BMI is lower than yours. The result adds the figure for men alone (26%) and women alone (31%); the next section has the full table.
BMI Prime: 1.00
Your BMI divided by 25, where overweight starts. 1.00 means you’re at that line; under 1.00 is below it.
Ponderal index: 14.7 kg/m³
Weight in kilograms divided by height in meters cubed. CDC and WHO publish no adult cut-offs for it, so compare it only with your own earlier results.
Weight milestones
The weight where each range starts at your height. At 5′7″: healthy from 118 lb, overweight from 160 lb, obesity from 192 lb, class 2 obesity from 224 lb and class 3 from 256 lb.
What-if table
Your BMI after losing or gaining 5 to 20 lb (2.5 to 10 kg in metric). Five pounds lighter, at 155 lb, the example’s BMI is 24.3.

How to calculate BMI by hand

In pounds and inches, multiply your weight by 703 and divide by your height in inches squared. In metric, divide your weight in kilograms by your height in meters squared. Both formulas are CDC’s (Calculating BMI); 703 turns pounds per square inch into kilograms per square meter.

  • US units: 160 lb at 5′7″ (67 in): 160 × 703 ÷ (67 × 67) = 112,480 ÷ 4,489 = 25.1.
  • Metric: 70 kg at 170 cm (1.70 m): 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2.
  • From centimeters: 70 ÷ 170 ÷ 170 × 10,000 = 24.2, the same result without converting to meters.

Round to one decimal before reading the category, as the calculator and the BMI charts do. The BMI formula guide explains where 703 comes from and the version Nick Trefethen proposed in 2013.

When BMI misleads

CDC calls BMI “a quick, safe, and reliable screening measure” and adds that it “does not distinguish between fat, muscle, and bone mass” (CDC). Four common cases:

  • Muscle. A muscular person can have a BMI over 25 with little body fat. The body fat calculator estimates body fat from tape or skinfold measurements, and BMI for athletes goes further.
  • Where fat sits. US guidelines treat a waist over 40 inches (102 cm) in men and 35 inches (88 cm) in women as a marker of higher risk, mainly at a BMI of 25 to 34.9 (NHLBI). The waist-to-height ratio is another check.
  • Age. The cut-offs stay the same at every adult age, but body fat at a given BMI also depends on age, sex and ethnic group (Gallagher et al., 2000), so the same BMI can mean a different amount of fat at 25 and at 75. The body fat percentage chart shows typical body fat by age.
  • Asian ancestry. A WHO expert consultation found that many Asian populations have higher risk at lower BMIs. It kept the international categories and identified BMI 23, 27.5, 32.5 and 37.5 as points for public-health action (WHO, 2004).

In 2025 the Lancet Commission on Clinical Obesity (58 experts, endorsed by 76 organizations) recommended confirming excess body fat with a body measurement such as waist size, or with a direct body-fat measurement, instead of diagnosing obesity from BMI alone. More in BMI limitations.

BMI and health

US guidelines link a BMI above the healthy range with higher risk of high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, coronary heart disease, stroke, sleep apnea and osteoarthritis (NHLBI). In a large trial of adults with overweight or obesity and type 2 diabetes, losing 5 to 10% of body weight was linked to better blood sugar, blood pressure, HDL cholesterol and triglycerides after one year (Wing et al., 2011).

CDC notes that “poor nutrition and underlying health conditions can result in underweight” (Health E-Stat 119); if your BMI is under 18.5 and you don’t know why, ask a doctor. For one person, CDC says BMI “should be considered with other factors”, such as blood pressure and cholesterol. The evidence behind each risk is in BMI and health risks.

Common situations

You want a version for women or men

The cut-offs are the same for everyone. The BMI calculator for women and the one for men add the waist cut-off for each sex and US data for women or men.

You’re 65 or older

The categories don’t change. The BMI calculator by age adds the research on BMI and survival in adults 65 and over.

You lift weights or play a power sport

Muscle can push BMI past 25. A waist measurement and a tape or skinfold estimate from the body fat calculator can suggest whether the extra weight is fat.

You want one target weight

The healthy range is every weight with a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 at your height. The ideal weight calculator shows the single weights that four published formulas give.

You use kilograms and centimeters

Switch the calculator to Metric. Results then show kilograms first, pounds second, and the healthy range in whole kilograms.

You want to lower your BMI

The what-if table shows your BMI at each 5 lb (2.5 kg) step. NIH’s Body Weight Planner builds a calorie and physical activity plan for a goal weight, for adults who aren’t pregnant or breastfeeding. More in how to lower your BMI.

Method. BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)², or lb × 703 ÷ in², rounded to one decimal; the category uses the rounded value. Metric input is computed in kg/m², US input with 703. Healthy limits are the lightest and heaviest whole pounds (or kilograms) whose rounded BMI is 18.5 to 24.9, each unit worked out on its own. The rank is the share of adults with a lower BMI (and, for “lower than”, the share with a higher one), read from the survey-weighted distribution of measured BMI, which CDC records to one decimal (1st to 99th percentile, linear interpolation between them); adults with the same BMI count on neither side. The category shares reproduce NCHS’s published crude rates: underweight 1.6%, overweight 32.1%, obesity 40.3%, class 3 obesity 9.4%. Data: CDC NHANES August 2021–August 2023.
