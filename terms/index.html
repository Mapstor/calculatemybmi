2. Description of Service

CalculateMyBMI.net provides free online Body Mass Index (BMI) calculators, body composition estimation tools, and educational health content designed to help users understand their weight status relative to established health guidelines. The Site is intended for general informational and educational purposes only.

Our services currently include the following calculators and tools:

  • Standard BMI Calculator — Calculates Body Mass Index using the standard WHO formula for adults, supporting both metric and imperial units.
  • BMI Calculator for Women — Provides BMI calculations with women-specific healthy weight ranges, body fat considerations, and life-stage guidance.
  • BMI Calculator for Men — Provides BMI calculations with men-specific healthy weight ranges and waist circumference guidelines.
  • BMI Calculator by Age — Delivers age-adjusted BMI interpretations and recommendations for different adult life stages.
  • Pediatric BMI Calculator — Calculates BMI percentiles for children and teens aged 2 to 19 using CDC growth chart data.
  • New BMI Calculator (Trefethen) — Computes BMI using the revised 2013 Trefethen formula that adjusts for height bias in the traditional formula.
  • Ideal Weight Calculator — Estimates ideal body weight using multiple established clinical formulas including Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi.
  • Lean Body Mass Calculator — Estimates lean body mass, fat mass, and body fat percentage using the Boer, James, and Hume formulas.

In addition to calculators, the Site provides educational guides and articles about BMI interpretation, healthy weight management, body composition, and related health topics. All calculations are performed client-side in your web browser; we do not store, transmit, or have access to the personal health measurements you enter into our calculators.

4. Use of Calculators

The BMI calculators and body composition tools provided on this Site generate results based on widely recognized mathematical formulas and publicly available health data. You acknowledge and agree to the following regarding the use of our calculators:

  • Estimates Only: All calculator results are estimates based on standardized formulas. They are not precise measurements of your individual health status, body fat percentage, or disease risk. Actual body composition and health outcomes vary significantly between individuals even at the same BMI value.
  • Limitations of BMI: BMI does not directly measure body fat. It does not account for differences in muscle mass, bone density, age, sex, ethnicity, or fat distribution. A person with high muscle mass (such as an athlete) may have a high BMI without having excess body fat. Conversely, a person with a normal BMI may have a high body fat percentage.
  • Not a Diagnosis: Calculator results do not constitute a medical diagnosis. A BMI classification of "overweight" or "obese" does not necessarily mean you are unhealthy, just as a "normal" BMI does not guarantee good health. Only a qualified healthcare professional can assess your individual health status.
  • General Information Only: The results and accompanying information are intended to provide general educational guidance. They should not be used as the sole basis for making health, dietary, fitness, or medical decisions.
  • Pediatric Use: The Pediatric BMI Calculator is designed for children and teens aged 2 to 19 and uses CDC growth chart percentiles. Results for children must always be interpreted by a pediatrician or qualified healthcare provider in the context of the child's individual growth pattern and development.
  • Accuracy of Input: The accuracy of calculator results depends on the accuracy of the data you enter. Incorrect height, weight, age, or sex inputs will produce inaccurate results. You are solely responsible for ensuring that the data you provide is correct.
